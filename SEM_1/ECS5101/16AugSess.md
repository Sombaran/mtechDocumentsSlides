## Alogithm techniques

- Brute force
  - Explore all possible solution with worrying about time and space complexity
- Back tracking*
  - In a systematic strategy, explore all possible solutions in a structures manner
- Greedy algorithm*
  - Operating by making locally optimal choices at each step
- Dynamic programming*
  - Divide a complex problem into simpler overlapping subproblems
- Divide and conquer*
  - Break complex problem in smaller sub problems, each sub problem is solved independently
- NP completeness*
  - Non polyomial algorithm
- Randomized alogithm
