## Blockchain proof of stake

## Definition

- Stakeholders of crypocurrency lock up certain amount of their coins as a collateral to become validators of new transactions and blocks
- Need to validate before earing rewards
- PoS reduces energy consumption by letting those who hold the coins secure the network
- Penalty for misbehaving and dishonestly and encourage network security and decentralization
- Costly and risky for malicious actors to control the majority of stake

## How it works

- Works in supply chain management
- `Proof of stake`  uses stake-based selection of validators to create and confirm blocks, reducing energy use and improving efficiency
- Blockchain provides decentralized, immutable ledger for supply chain transactions, enchances transparency and tracibilty
- Smart contracts on `Proof of stake` blockchains automate workflows like payment, improving automation and reducing cost
- Advanced PoS variants address fairness, privacy, and scalibilty in complex supply chain ecosystem

## Benifits

- Transparency
- Tracibility
- Efficiency
- Mitigated counterfeit risks
- Secure and Immuntable data
- Streamlined compliance and auditing

## Challenges

- Integration issues
- Scalibility
- Data privacy and confidentiality
- Governance and consensus mechanism
- Standards and operability
- Costs and RoI considerations
- Education and skill gap
