## Binary search tree

- Popular for removing duplicate entry
- search, delete and insertion is log n
- left child value is less than root which is further less than right (L< Root< Right)
- During traversal the above rule can be neglected but during insertion the above rule may cause discrepancy therefore we need to adjust it accordingly
